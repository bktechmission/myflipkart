How these are measured

Per conversation (cid + sid): handoff = customer asked for a human; escalation = a guardrail sent it to a human (e.g. refund over the limit); deflection = no human involved; resolution = deflected and the task got done (order action committed or thumbs-up) with no thumbs-down; recontact = a closed conversation followed by another one from the same customer within 72h (proxy for "didn't really solve it"); CSAT = thumbs-up share of feedback, which Sauby asks for after every resolution, escalation and handoff.
